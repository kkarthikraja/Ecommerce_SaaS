# 📊 E-commerce SaaS Metrics Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview

This project showcases an advanced **Power BI dashboard** developed for an e-commerce SaaS client to analyze performance metrics across customer acquisition, revenue trends, churn behavior, and product monetization. The goal was to provide strategic visibility into subscription health and guide data-driven decisions.

## 💡 Key Features

- **Revenue Trend Analysis**  
  Track monthly and yearly revenue (MRR & ARR) from 2021 to 2025, highlighting growth periods and downturns.

- **Customer Health Metrics**  
  Visualize customer acquisition, churn, and retention to identify high-risk segments and long-term patterns.

- **Subscription Performance KPIs**  
  - ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)  
  - Net Revenue Retention (NRR)  
  - Existing vs. New Customer Revenue Contribution

- **Product Variant Insights**  
  Understand revenue contributions by product types such as:
  - Adobe One Pro (AOP)
  - AOP for Camera Brand A/B/C
  - AOP (Student version)

- **Business Commentary Layer**  
  Strategic narrative built into the report to provide interpretation and recommendations for key trends.

## 📈 Business Impact

This dashboard enabled the client to:

- Recognize revenue saturation and sharp post-2023 decline  
- Uncover high customer churn in 2024 and 2025  
- Re-evaluate pricing and value proposition for different product segments  
- Shift strategic focus from acquisition to retention

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **DAX** for measures and KPIs
- **SQL** for data transformation and preparation
- **Google Analytics** data source

## 📎 Use Case

This solution supports:

- **SaaS product teams** evaluating customer and product performance
- **Growth analysts** monitoring monetization trends
- **Finance teams** tracking recurring revenue dynamics
